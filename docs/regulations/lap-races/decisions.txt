Lap races: who counts in the cup
================================

2026-11-24, the maintainer: in a lap race (XCO) the stage rule "time" put riders a lap
down ahead of those who rode every lap, and that is wrong. In a real race a rider
either has every lap or is DNF, and then the stage does not count for them. So the cup
counts only riders who have, at the moment, exactly as many laps as their group's
leader -- for a group standing -- and as the absolute leader for an absolute one.

Left open by that word, answered by the maintainer on 2026-11-24:

- The finish protocol generator writes the attempts of a Number of Tries race in the
  columns it writes laps in, and a rider there may have a result without every attempt:
  a stage has a checkbox for the rule, ticked by default, to untick for Number of Tries.
- The cup has no absolute standing beside the group ones: laps are compared with the
  group's leader, and with the absolute leader only where a protocol has no groups.

Read as: the leader is whoever has the most laps among the category's results, as the
protocol ranks laps first (a disqualified or unfinished rider leads nobody); a rider
short of them has no result in that stage, as one who did not finish, and the build's
log names them.
