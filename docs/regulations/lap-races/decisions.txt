Lap races: who counts in the cup
================================

2026-11-24, the maintainer: in a lap race (XCO) the stage rule "time" put riders a lap
down ahead of those who rode every lap, and that is wrong. In a real race a rider
either has every lap or is DNF, and then the stage does not count for them. So the cup
counts only riders who have, at the moment, exactly as many laps as their group's
leader -- for a group standing -- and as the absolute leader for an absolute one.

Left open by that word, answered by the maintainer on 2026-11-24:

- The finish protocol generator writes the attempts of a Number of Tries race in the
  columns it writes laps in, and a rider there may have a result without every attempt:
  a stage has a checkbox for the rule, ticked by default, to untick for Number of Tries.
- The cup has no absolute standing beside the group ones: laps are compared with the
  group's leader, and with the absolute leader only where a protocol has no groups.

2026-11-24, the maintainer, asked who leads at a moment of a live race, when the finish
protocol generator lists a rider still on course as DNF (laps, no result) and a lapped
rider given a finish mark already has a result: the leader on the course -- whoever has
the most laps now, finished or not; a disqualified rider leads nobody. So a lapped rider
who has finished is not counted while the leader rides on, and nobody of a group counts
until its leader finishes.

Read as: a rider short of the leader's laps has no result in that stage, as one who did
not finish, and the build's log names them. The protocol lists a rider who abandoned as
DNF too, alike with one still on course, so he leads as well: a final protocol counts as
it did before that answer, unless a rider who abandoned rode more laps than every
finisher of his group -- then nobody of the group counts. The maintainer's rule has no
such finishers (a rider has every lap or is DNF).
