Sum of times: the stage rule "time" and the cup rule "sum_of_times"
=====================================================================

2026-11-18, the maintainer: the result of every stage is its time; the cup's result is
the sum of the stages' times. The cup's protocol shows every stage's result in a column
of its own.

Left open by that word, answered by the defaults the stub rules already had, and
confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-11-19:

- Somebody without a time in a stage (not started, not finished, disqualified, missing
  from a sheet) has no sum: listed after the ranked, without a place, who missed fewer
  stages first.

2026-11-19, the maintainer: several stages may be missed. By default somebody who
missed a stage has no place, but a broadcast needs an option to show everybody with a
place: those who missed one stage rank among themselves after those who have every
stage, those who missed two after them, three after those. Read as: within each, by
the sum of the stages they have; somebody with no stage at all shares the last place,
with no total.
- Equal sums share a place; the next place is skipped (1, 2, 2, 4). The shared place
  keeps the order the athletes are listed in. No tie-break.
- The total is written as a time: h:mm:ss from an hour on, m:ss below it, with as many
  decimals as the most precise of the athlete's stage results is written with.
